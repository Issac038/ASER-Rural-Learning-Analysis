# ASER Data Analysis — Key Insights

## 1. Learning outcomes fluctuate over time

Learning outcomes show no consistent upward trend. There was a sharp decline from 2014 to 2016, a slight recovery in 2018, another decline by 2022, followed by improvement in 2024.

**Evidence:** Power BI Page 1 — Overall Trend.

## 2. Arithmetic is weaker than Reading

Arithmetic performance is consistently lower than Reading, with an approximately 13 percentage-point difference between the two subjects.

**Evidence:** Power BI Page 2 — Reading vs Arithmetic.

## 3. Private schools maintain higher learning outcomes than Government schools

Private schools started considerably higher than Government schools. In 2014, Private schools were approximately 71.63%, compared with 48.81% for Government schools. Both experienced a major decline by 2016.

**Evidence:** Power BI Page 1 — Overall Trend.

## 4. Arithmetic becomes particularly weak at higher standards

The Standard-level analysis shows that Arithmetic performance is particularly weak at higher standards, with Standard 5 Arithmetic standing out as a major weakness.

**Evidence:** Power BI Page 3 — Standard Analysis.

## 5. Learning outcomes improved between 2022 and 2024

Both Government and Private schools showed improvement between 2022 and 2024, with Government schools showing a somewhat sharper recovery.

**Evidence:** Power BI Page 1 — Overall Trend.

# High-Impact Problem

Students in rural India can advance to higher grades while still lacking foundational arithmetic and reading skills expected at those levels. The ASER data shows persistent learning gaps, particularly in arithmetic, even among students in higher standards.

## Target User

**Primary User:** Government school teachers.

**Secondary User:** Education Department officials.

## Beneficiary

Students struggling with foundational arithmetic and reading skills.

## Proposed Solution

A data-driven education management platform for teachers and Education Department officials. The platform uses learning assessment data and historical ASER trends to identify foundational learning gaps, monitor progress, and support targeted educational interventions.
